Who it's for

Writers in Pakistan, or of Pakistani origin, writing in English

Wherever you live, if you are Pakistani or have Pakistani roots and you are working on fiction, poetry, memoir, essays or other narrative non-fiction, this group is for you. All work is shared and discussed in English.

The ground rules

How the circle works

  • It is free.No fees, no subscriptions, no upsell.
  • We meet once a month, online, on Microsoft Teams.The last Tuesday of each month, 9:00 to 10:30pm Pakistan time (PKT, UTC+5). If you're abroad, please check what that is where you live.
  • Creative writing and narrative non-fiction only.Fiction, poetry, memoir, personal essay, literary journalism. Not academic, business or technical writing.
  • English only.All submissions and readings are in English.
  • You read your work aloud.Members read an extract from their work and the group responds.
  • Feedback can be very critical, but it must be constructive.We say what isn't working and suggest how it could work better.

Hard on the page, kind to the writer.

Sharing work

Your words stay yours

Readers are encouraged to share a copy of the extract they are reading, so others can follow along and give precise feedback. By joining, every member agrees that:

  • Extracts shared in the group are used for critique only.
  • Members will not copy, quote, publish, share or reuse anyone else's extract for any other purpose.
Code of conduct

What every member agrees to

By joining Qissa Circle, each member agrees to the following.

  1. Attendance. Members are expected to attend at least 50% of sessions. Members who fall below this may be asked to leave, at the discretion of the organiser or, once it is formed, the committee.
  2. Work in progress. Members must have a piece of work in progress and are expected to offer to read at 90% of the sessions they attend. The chair decides who reads at each session, fairly and without favouritism, so not every member will get to read at every session.
  3. Giving feedback. Give feedback that is critical where it needs to be, and always constructive: say what isn't working and how it could be better.
  4. Receiving feedback. Listen to feedback on your own work with the aim of considering how it could improve.
  5. Other people's writing. Never use anyone else's writing for any purpose other than critique within the group.
  6. Sharing your text. Share a copy of your extract with the group in advance, or share it on screen while you read.
  7. Cameras. Cameras are optional. Keep yours off unless you are comfortable having it on.
  8. What we read. Only fiction and narrative non-fiction.
  9. Preaching and hate speech. No preaching, proselytising or hate speech, unless it is part of a character's persona or integral to the storyline. Writing about religion and faith is welcome.
  10. Sexual content. No writing centred on explicit sexual content. Anything of the kind you would find in a novel published in Europe is acceptable, provided it is not gratuitous.
  11. We critique the writing, not the ideology. This is not a political forum. A character may hold political, religious or other views that some readers find offensive or extreme. We comment only on the quality of the writing and its coherence, never on whether we agree with the character.
  12. Chairing sessions. Bilal Ghafoor will chair sessions at first. Once the committee is formed, it will choose who chairs later sessions.
  13. Admission. At the start, admission is decided by Bilal Ghafoor.
  14. Running the group. Within the first six months, a committee will be formed to make decisions about how the group is run. It will be chosen by vote of members who have attended at least 50% of the sessions held up to the date of the vote.
  15. Free forever. Attending the group online will always be free.
Joining

Entry is by a sample of up to 500 words

  1. Choose an extract of creative writing or narrative non-fiction, in English, of up to 500 words.
  2. Email it as a Microsoft Word or PDF attachment to the address below, with your name and a line about your Pakistani connection. Submissions are by email only.
  3. By submitting, you agree to the code of conduct. If it's a fit, you'll receive the joining details for the next session. The first session is on Tuesday 26 January 2027.
